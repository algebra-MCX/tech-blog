**Projects are the thread: use code, experiments and debugging records to answer concrete questions.**

## Choose your starting point

- **New to the topic** → [Quickstarts](/guides/sections/quickstarts/). Run a small Python example; the first ones need no extra packages or GPU.
- **Solving a problem** → [Projects](/projects/). Browse experiments, debugging and design, or go straight to [Search](/search/).
- **Interested in my judgment** → [Notes](/notes/). Perspectives and choices across research, learning, investing, life and personal finance.

## Follow a project

**Overview → Start → Articles → Code.**

Check the question and scope, fill in the background you need, then read the work and inspect its implementation. The directory separates **My projects** from **Open-source contributions**, with links to upstream PRs.

## Read the evidence

**Check the evidence before applying a conclusion.** Look at the version, environment, comparison and measurement boundary. A **Working outline** records hypotheses or plans that still need validation.
